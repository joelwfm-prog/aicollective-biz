Tell us your goal

Tell us what you want AI to do for your business.

You don't need to know the tool, the jargon, or the tech. Just describe the outcome you want in plain English. We'll map out a practical solution — using tools that already exist, or a custom setup built for you.

1. Describe it

In the box below, tell us the annoying task, the goal, or the "I wish AI could…" in your own words. One sentence or ten — whatever it takes.

2. We map it

We match your goal to the right approach — an off-the-shelf tool, a simple automation, or a custom build. Honest about what's worth paying for.

3. You decide

You get a plain-language plan back. Run it yourself, or have us set it up. No pressure, no obligation — asking is free.

The free-text box

What do you want AI to do?

Be as specific or as vague as you like. Real examples: "Stop me chasing late invoices," "Show up when people ask ChatGPT for a Regina electrician," "Turn my one blog post into a week of social posts."

The more detail, the better the solution we can map for you.
So we can send your solution map back. We never sell your info.
Good to know

Common questions

Does it cost anything to ask?

No. Describing your goal and getting an initial solution map is free. If a paid tool or custom build makes sense, we'll tell you upfront — no surprises.

Do I need to know which tool I need?

Not at all. Just describe the outcome you want. Figuring out the right tool or setup is our job, not yours.

Who's this for?

Small and medium businesses in Saskatchewan and across Western Canada that want practical AI help without enterprise jargon or a big budget.

What happens to my info?

Your description comes straight to us so we can reply. We never sell your data, and you only join the newsletter if you tick the box.
