About the Collective

Prairie-made AI, without the hype.

The AI Collective exists to make artificial intelligence make sense for the businesses that keep Western Canada running — the shops, trades, cafés, clinics, and farms that don't have a tech department.

Why we started this

Most AI coverage is written for Silicon Valley, not Saskatchewan. It's either breathless hype or dense jargon — and neither helps a busy owner decide what to actually do on Monday morning.

We're operators and builders based in Moose Jaw. We use AI every day to run real businesses, and we got tired of translating the noise ourselves. So we started sharing what works — plainly, honestly, and with a Prairie accent.

Our promise: no hype, no fear-mongering, no affiliate-link soup. When a tool is worth your money — including ours — we'll tell you why. When it isn't, we'll say that too.

  • Local first

    Built for Sask and Western Canada — real examples, real grants, real regulations like PIPEDA.

  • Plain English

    If your teenager couldn't follow it, we rewrite it. No jargon walls.

  • Practical over trendy

    We care about what saves you time and makes you money — not what's viral this week.

  • Honest, always

    We build AI tools ourselves, so we'll always be upfront about what's ours and why we recommend it.
