# CASL Launch Playbook — The AI Collective Newsletter

Plain-English guide to launching the newsletter **without breaking Canada's
anti-spam law (CASL)**. Not legal advice — but it reflects the CRTC's published
rules. When in doubt on a big list, confirm with a lawyer.

---

## The one rule that changes everything
**In Canada you need consent BEFORE you email. You cannot email strangers and
let them opt out.** That's the US model (CAN-SPAM), and it's illegal here.

Two kinds of consent count:

### 1. Express consent (the gold standard)
Someone proactively signed up — checked a box, filled the form, requested the
lead magnet. Never expires until they unsubscribe. **This is what our site form
collects.** Build the whole list on this and you're safe.

### 2. Implied consent (narrow, time-limited)
- **Existing business relationship (EBR):** someone who bought from you (or
  made an inquiry) — valid **2 years** after a purchase / **6 months** after an
  inquiry. Your existing clients qualify → you can email them now.
- **Conspicuously published email:** a business that posts `info@` publicly with
  no "no marketing" note — and your message must relate to their role. Narrow
  and legally risky as a list-building strategy. Don't lean on it.

**Buying/scraping a list and blasting it = not allowed.** Penalties run up to
**$1M per violation (individual)** and **$10M (business)**, and each email can be
a separate violation. It also destroys your sender reputation, which junks the
domain you're trying to build.

---

## Every commercial email you send must have (all three):
1. **Consent** (express or valid implied) — logged.
2. **Clear identification** — who you are (The AI Collective) + a real mailing
   address + contact (Saskatchewan business address is fine).
3. **Working unsubscribe** — one-click, honoured within **10 business days**.
   Resend adds a List-Unsubscribe header automatically; also put a visible
   "unsubscribe" link in the footer (our template already references one).

## Your compliant launch plan

### Step 1 — Turn the site into a consent machine (already scaffolded)
- The newsletter form + intake form capture **express consent**.
- Add a clear line at the signup: *"Yes, email me The AI Collective Weekly. One
  email a week, unsubscribe anytime."* (unchecked box or explicit button — no
  pre-ticked boxes).
- **Log proof of consent:** date/time, email, what they agreed to, and where
  (which form/page). Store it. If the CRTC ever asks, the burden of proof is on
  YOU. (Next build step: I can add consent-logging to the backend so every
  signup is recorded.)

### Step 2 — Seed the list legitimately
You do NOT start from zero:
- **Existing clients** (Results AI / your other businesses): EBR implied consent
  — you can email them now. Best practice: your first email asks them to confirm
  they want The AI Collective Weekly (converts implied → express).
- **People you know / networking / Chamber contacts** who've said "yes, add me":
  express consent — get it in writing (even a text/email "yes please").
- **Lead magnet:** the free AEO checklist on the site → email required to get it
  → they've opted in. This is your growth engine.

### Step 3 — The confirmation (double opt-in) — recommended
When someone signs up, send ONE confirmation email: "Click to confirm your
subscription." Only add confirmed addresses to the sending list. This:
- Proves consent beyond doubt.
- Keeps your list clean → better deliverability.
- Note: the confirmation email itself is allowed (it's a response to their
  request), but keep it purely transactional — no marketing pitch inside it.

### Step 4 — Send
- From-name: **The AI Collective**. From-address: `hello@intheresults.com`
  (Option B, now) or `info@aicollective.biz` (Option A, after Resend upgrade).
- Cadence: one email every Thursday (matches the site promise).
- Every send: identification + working unsubscribe. Honour unsubs immediately.

## Quick do / don't
| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Email people who signed up | Email a bought/scraped list |
| Email existing clients (EBR, 2 yrs) | Pre-tick the consent box |
| Log date + source of every consent | Hide who you are |
| One-click unsubscribe, honoured fast | Ignore unsubscribes past 10 days |
| Confirm implied → express when you can | Put marketing in the confirmation email |

## Suggested first sends
1. **To existing clients (implied consent):** short note — "We started a weekly
   AI newsletter for Prairie business. Want in? [Confirm]." Converts them to
   express.
2. **Public launch:** drive traffic to the site's signup / lead magnet (social,
   Chamber, word of mouth). Everyone who signs up = express consent.
3. **Issue #1** ("Nobody's Googling you anymore") goes to the confirmed list.

You already have Issue #1 written and the 5-week calendar. The list just has to
be built the right way — opt-in, logged, unsubscribable.
