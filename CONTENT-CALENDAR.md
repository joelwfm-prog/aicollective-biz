## Why this order

1. **Lead with visibility (AEO).** It's the highest-anxiety, highest-curiosity topic for owners right now ("am I invisible to ChatGPT?") and it's the site's signature angle. Strong hook for issue #1.
2. **Then time-savings (automation).** Once they care, show a fast, tangible win.
3. **Then money (grants).** Timely, concrete, Prairie-specific — great for shares and forwards.
4. **Then consistency (social/content).** For the businesses now bought in and wanting a system.
5. **Close the month on trust (ground rules / PIPEDA).** Positions the Collective as the honest, responsible voice — sets up the paid/tool conversations later.

## Newsletter format (repeatable template)

Every Thursday issue follows the same skimmable shape — keeps writing fast and readers trained:

1. **One-line hook** (the subject line, repeated as the opener).
2. **The read (≈250–350 words)** — this week's main idea in plain English, one Prairie example.
3. **Do this now** — a single concrete action the reader can take in under 30 minutes.
4. **One link** — to the week's anchor playbook/article on aicollective.biz.
5. **From the Prairies** — a short, human sign-off (local note, event, or aside).
6. **CASL footer** — who we are, why they're getting this, one-click unsubscribe.

**Voice:** like a sharp friend who runs a business too. Short sentences. No "unlock," "leverage," "supercharge," or emoji soup.
