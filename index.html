Start here

New to AI? Do these four things first.

You don't need a strategy deck. You need a weekend and a coffee. Here's where every Prairie business should start.

1

Make yourself findable by AI

AI answer engines are the new front door. Make sure your business shows up when someone asks ChatGPT or Google's AI for a recommendation in your area.

2

Kill one repetitive task

Pick the most annoying, repeatable thing you do every week. There's almost certainly an AI or automation that handles 80% of it.

3

Get consistent on social

Consistency beats brilliance. Use AI to keep your feed alive without it becoming a second full-time job.

4

Set your ground rules

Decide what AI can and can't touch in your business — customer data, client work, anything sensitive — before your team runs ahead of you.
